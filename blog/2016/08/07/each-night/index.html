7 August 2016 Poetry

Each night

Each night she comes in

And cuts my heart out of my chest

She spends the evening making me remember

And fills me with regret
Each night I lay there

Dreaming of who we were

I can dream of nothing else

But the days I spent with her
And each night just before the dawn

She comes and sews me back together

She leaves again to return at night

So I can remember how I loved her
