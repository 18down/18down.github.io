15 July 2016 Poetry

16:03

In your beauty, I learnt to love

And time no longer had any sway

In your shape, I had found my peace

Now all I know has gone away

 

In your words, I had a purpose

Deeper than any ocean or deep blue sea

In yours arms I had want for nothing

But now all love has forsaken me

 

Though I searched, and pleaded

Again this joy I could not attain

With desperate longing I wait

To find this love again
