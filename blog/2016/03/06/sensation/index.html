6 March 2016 Poetry

Sensation

Sensations like violent dreams
torn open eyes wide to see
Inconsolable and worn
this terrible tragedy

flesh ripped from bone
conquered by our own vice
a wheel, a broken cog
in some universal device

understanding the reason we hold on
fingers loosed from their rest
necks twisted round to watch
life, hope, all we detest
