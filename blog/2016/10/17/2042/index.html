17 October 2016 Poetry

20:42

You can have my words

Useless things after all

You can steal my drama

I don't long for it anymore

 

I'm down there in the nowhere

cellphones ringing without end

we can take this road I know

but I don't have the strength to pretend

 

So utterly numb and confused

nowhere left to go

unfriend, dead end, pretend

which way, I don't know

#emptiness #life #longing #loss #poetry
