22 October 2018 Poetry

22 October 2018

They come to me for answers

But I really know nothing

I'm grimacing at the world

I'm not smiling, I hate everything

They think I'm a man

I'm a boy in a broken shell

I keep staring at their faces

Too stupid to see they're in hell

I'm losing what's left of my mind

I'm trying to hard to stay sane

Keep pointing those fingers

I'm the easy one to blame

I'm king of the ashes

I'm master of the dirt

Take what's left of me

I don't care if it hurts
