1 March 2018 Poetry

1 March 2018

Don't try comfort me

As if you'd know what to say

Don't you dare promise me

When I won't believe you anyway

I traced the lines on your face

I stared until I went some other place

I realise there are things in this world

You can never ever replace

I don't need it anymore

The air feels like a cold grey

I was walking for days

And I just lost my way
