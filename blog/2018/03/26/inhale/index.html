26 March 2018 Poetry

Inhale

I have inhaled all those I have loved

Till I choked on their memory

They turned into vapour

And simply dissapeared before me

All I have known and wanted

Simply turned to dust

Even those I thought of gold

Are uneven shades of rust

And so the years are a spiral

Further down they go

All the desires I ever had

You shall never know
