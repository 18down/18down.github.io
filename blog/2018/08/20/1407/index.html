20 August 2018 Poetry

20 August 2018

I'm sorry for the ghosts

Now it's all just a memory

Only one of us was real

And that was me

Even if the war is won

How many do we leave dead

Could we rather not have made peace

But we killed each other instead

It's just words

And yet I have nothing to say

Just like everyone else

You too will go away

I didn't start this

But I know how it will end

Let's just be real for once

Let's not pretend
