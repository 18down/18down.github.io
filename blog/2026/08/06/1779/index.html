6 August 2026 Poetry

6 August 2026

I still worry

The big things, the small things

The meaning of life

Will I be in heaven or hell?

Is there a Mrs right?

Worrying endlessly

About the passage of time

Is someone telling the truth

Or is just another lie

Do I matter?

Does anything at all?

I worry when I don’t worry

Till I worry all the more
