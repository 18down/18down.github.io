25 January 2015 Poetry

If

If I could turn back time
And change all that has been
Yet that is an impossibility
What lessons would i learn
If all that was changed
Who then would i be?

I have learnt that my will is wicked
That my truths are made in flesh
That nothing I do is worthy
I have tried making my plans
And all have turned to naught
And now, it's not up to me
