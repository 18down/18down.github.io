9 November 2015 Poetry

The ghost

I'm dreaming of a ghost
Yet I'm the one that died
She's off living life
And I'm dead inside
You remember me at all?
I have moved but still you stay
In all the dark of my mind
I don't want to fight anymore
The whole world has gone away
And left me cold and blind
You thought no one was watching
But I saw just who you were
You were everything I ever wanted
Yeah, I think you were her
