27 November 2015 Poetry

20:07

Time doesn't heal a thing
Just dulls the tremendous ache
Of a life I never got to live

Drowning in the memories
Dying another day in the past
With no one left to forgive

She set on me on fire
And left me smoldering
Walking away from me

I'd watch her sleeping
Afraid she'd be gone if she woke
And so it has come to be
