21 July 2015 Poetry

Anymore

All 6 guitars hang on the wall Strings broken, dusty I don't want to play anymore I always thought there was more So very much more than this But everything is worthless I run this way, then that I wake up from dreams That just won't let me forget Time, the seconds decay My skin, like brittle bones of birds Lost and adrift in a sea of words They mean nothing Turning into ash in my mouth Like love I live without When you've been wading in blood Unable to find a promised shore There is only never, not anymore
