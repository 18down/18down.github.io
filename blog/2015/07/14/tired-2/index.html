14 July 2015 Poetry

Tired

Tired of wasting my breath
Buried in the dreams of another life
Smothered by the loss and spite

I'm standing at the edge
Of this great divide
At the point of falling off the side

I've been breaking for a year and a half
I've been dying with a broken heart
And my prayers are fading fast

I am but a sum of my sins
Transgressions buried in my skin
I'm ending, only wanting to begin
