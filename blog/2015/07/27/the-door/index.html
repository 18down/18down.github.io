27 July 2015 Poetry

The door

You never cried after I was gone
I always just darkened your door
See love doesn't fade for me
I just can't use it anymore

You're eyes once smiled
When you looked at me
Now that beautiful face
Is nothing but a memory

Oh God let me forget
Let me have some kind of future
I'm so lost and alone
Why can't I find her?
