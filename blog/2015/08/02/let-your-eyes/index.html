2 August 2015 Poetry

Let your eyes

Let your eyes see me
Let my heart not sigh in the breaking dawn
Let all things beautiful be
Restored after the destruction of the storm

Let hope fill the halls
Of my dreams again
Let the earth not swallow me
Buried in an unmarked grave

If only my enemies were more than apathy
And my desires greater than my fears
If I could close my eyes and rest
Just make everything quietly dissapear

No more faces in the streets
No laughter, cars, skies or trees
No wants or needs, just you and me
Someone I do not know
