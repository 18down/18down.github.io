24 August 2015 Poetry

Gone

The more I think
The less I feel
There are no more tears
Or hurts to conceal

The absence of love
Hasn't made me strong
It's made me lament
All that is gone

All that you have seen
Is just something that could have been
It's a beautiful world
But it's ugly to me
