5 May 2015 Poetry

16:08

You would be perfect
And I'd be a fuckup
You would be the one I'd choose
And I'd be all broken up
Its just one thing
I wish I could say
That's all it is
That keeps me away
And nothing you'd think
I find myself in hell
And I know what I feel
But there's no way I can tell

I deserve your disdain
