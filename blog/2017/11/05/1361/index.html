5 November 2017 Poetry

5 November 2017

I’m watching the world

It makes very little sense

All these Sunday afternoon “get togethers”

Seem little more than an annoyance

All the parties and travels

Places I just don’t desire to see

Here on the outskirts of society

Seems a better place to be

I have no idea

Other than here I don’t belong

But where could I possibly go

Where humanity hasn’t already gone?
