2 November 2017 Poetry

Roads

We’re just stains on the wall

Shadows where we used to be

Empty rooms breathe a sigh

Like the emptiness deep in me

We’re just crumpled sheets

Desolate beds and broken dreams

The ticking of a clock on the wall

It’s all gone like it’s never been
