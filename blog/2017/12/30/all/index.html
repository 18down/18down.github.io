30 December 2017 Poetry

All

<pre>All the pieces tattered and torn
trying to make myself ok
buried down at the bottom

smashed the dreams I'll start again
getting lost been thrown away
this is all I wanted

Trying to get back from where I'm from
the better I get the worse I become</pre>
