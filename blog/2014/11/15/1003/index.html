15 November 2014 Poetry

10:03

Has all the evidence been destroyed
I thought I made perfectly sure
Yet it keeps appearing again
I can't rid myself of the ghost of her
It feels like it was just a dream
None of it ever happened at all
Still i know I was there
And i am not there anymore
