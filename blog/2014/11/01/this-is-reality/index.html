1 November 2014 Poetry

This is reality

I'd like to wake up
Smell her hair
As she's laying there
Feel her breathing softly
Her the breath move her lips
Close, not moving, not even gently

The texture of her skin
The lines of her neck
The way he eyelashes curl up
The feeling of the sheets
Light breaking in the dawn
This is so much more than enough

Feel her fingers lock in mine
Bending, squeezing in that sleepy way
I'd stay here forever, if only I was brave
I don't want to move, the moment might pass
Perhaps it's just a dream as it's always been
Something that was never made to last
