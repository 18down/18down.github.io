4 November 2014 Poetry

4 November 2014

I say things here
Simply because they have nowhere else to go
I realise that I am a fool
But that's what everyone already knows
I don't want to fall in love
It's a fear into longer wish to bare
It always seems so easy
But that's of course when you don't really care
