4 November 2014 Poetry

Hope

If hope was measured in set amounts
It would seem that I have spent my last
What use is dreaming of a future
When the present just becomes the past
Such beautiful moments
Mired in the failure of my tongue
I would think that I was breathing
But alas, I have an iron lung
Such a tiresome disposition
A dreadful malady
I simply shudder at the thought
That I would need to be me
