8 November 2014 Poetry

09:02

Here we stand
Mouths full of saliva and disease
Devouring the hearts of others
Here we fall
Shattered upon our knees
Pleading with our dead lovers

Here we dream
In a lifeless, starless night
Eyes sewn shut to never see
Here we awake
In the storms of a lost life
Drifting, wandering aimlessly
