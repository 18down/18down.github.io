9 November 2014 Poetry

All our secrets

All our secrets
Buried behind our eyes
Dreams untold
Tell all our lies

I'm a lecherous worm
Forgotten with a broken crown
For once we'll see the unburdened
When the world comes crashing down

We've come so far
The ingested shit and bile
Coming forth from a toothless mouth
Limping the final mile

Shattered like glass
All we are is taken to the grave
I desire my freedom
But cannot stop being a slave

The words spilling like water
Just to be wiped away
I am so lost my my friend
What the fuck am I to say?
