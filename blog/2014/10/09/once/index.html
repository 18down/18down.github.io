9 October 2014 Uncategorised

Once

Once I swore I had wings
But I've burnt them all away
I believe I had a voice
But now there's nothing left to say
I gave of my everything
And I was left here to bleed
So I'm sitting here waiting
Till the last bit is taken from me
Im nothing, I'm just another man
Nothing special, no good news
I don't even want to win anymore
Because that means I will lose
