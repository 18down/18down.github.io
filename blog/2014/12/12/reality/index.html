12 December 2014 Poetry

Reality

None of this is depression
Just seeing the world as it is
Human evolution failing
Drowning in shit and piss

War, famine, political lies
Murder, rape, marital demise
Capitalistic greed and plunder
A man being another man's bride

Open your eyes, see as it has become
We're politically correct with nowhere to run
Freedom of speech yet no freedom at all
If this is it i dont want it anymore

The starving have less
The wealthy have more
Beating our wives and children
Stealing because we are bored

Socially connected
Visible at every turn
Yeah some of us
Just want to watch the world burn
