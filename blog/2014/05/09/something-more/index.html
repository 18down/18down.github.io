Something more

What have I become
I am so afraid to see
Who is this bitter man
Standing in front of me

Isn't there something
So much more
I don't want to think about you
Anymore
The distance between is
Is greater than the stars
Could I close my eyes
And forget about who we are

I thought love was one thing
Till I washed out to your sea
So I closed my eyes and prayed to drown
As your waves crashed over me
