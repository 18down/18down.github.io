21 July 2014 Uncategorised

Down

The world of shit around my knees
The bleeding edges of my eyes
No more tears left in this head
And it's really not a surprise

I've been down so long
I forgot how to stand
It's ok here in the gutter
Maybe that was always the plan

The whipping hasn't ever stopped
The people telling me everything I'm not
The hell is where I'm from
Is the hell that is all I've got

I don't dream anymore
Since the darks fills that too
It's the price of a life unlived
And finding that the lies are true
